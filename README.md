# ⚔️ Anime Crusaders X: Smart Farm

A smart auto farm for Anime Crusaders X on Roblox. It plans what the game's own Auto Play runs next: story, legend stages, challenges, raids, portals and Expeditions. It claims the free rewards, builds your team, evolves the units you pick and farms what they need.

```lua
loadstring(game:HttpGet("https://raw.githubusercontent.com/headshot7535-png/Anime-Crusaders-X/main/AnimeCrusadersX_SmartFarm.lua"))()
```

- Never spends Robux.
- Every switch is in the window, in plain words. Gems are only spent on what you switch on.
